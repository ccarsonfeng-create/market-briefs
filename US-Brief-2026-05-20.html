🎯 Interview Prep — 6 Questions (Expanded Format)

1 "10Y at 52-week highs but VIX sub-19 — what's the disconnect?"
Rates/Macro

It's actually not a disconnect — it's the textbook stagflation signature. Rate vol (MOVE ~127) is the leading indicator; equity vol (VIX 18.4) is the lagger that only catches up when the rate move starts breaking either earnings expectations or credit. We're not there yet because credit confirms the bond move is orderly: HY OAS at 285bps, IG at 80bps — both at 6-month tights. If those start widening alongside yields, that's when VIX wakes up.

The other piece is that 10Y at 4.60% isn't being driven by growth fears — it's term premium and inflation premium. 10Y breakeven 2.45%, TIPS real yield 2.15% — both elevated. That's a "higher for longer" curve, not a "higher and recession" curve. Equity multiples can survive that, especially with NVDA-style earnings still delivering.

VIX wakes up if HY OAS breaks 320bps or 10Y breaks 4.80% — until then, the disconnect is rational, not complacent.
Data cited: MOVE 127 · VIX 18.4 · HY OAS 285bps · IG 80bps · 10Y 4.60% · breakeven 2.45% · TIPS real 2.15%
2 "Why hasn't BoJ intervened yet at 158.90?"
FX/EM

Two reasons. First, MoF cares about rate-of-change, not absolute level. The move from 156 to 158.90 has taken ~10 sessions — that's controlled appreciation, not the disorderly spike that triggered the April 30 + May 1 rounds. Suzuki and Kanda are watching for a discontinuous jump, not a slow grind. Second, optics matter — they prefer to intervene at round numbers (159 or 160) because the headline effect on positioning is larger.

What's telling is that 1M USD/JPY vol is up 0.3 vols and risk-reversals have flipped to JPY-calls — the options market is positioning for intervention. Trade desks estimate a $30-50B intervention size per round; MoF used ~$60B across the April-May rounds combined. The 159.30-159.50 zone is where dealers think the next strike lands.

If we get a 159 print on a single candle without macro news, that's the BoJ trigger — I'd be short USD/JPY 158.40 with stop at 160.30 to position for it.
